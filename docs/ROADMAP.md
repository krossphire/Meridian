# Roadmap

Core controller operation is the product priority: a solid connection, an accurate heading, click or type to turn, stop, and reconnect. That is what 0.1.0-alpha2 is for.

Replacing the Green Heron RT-21 Setup Utility (4.B12) comes after this. No RT-21 owner should need that utility, but the inventory of its settings, the command map, and the risk of each write are written up only after alpha2 ships, in `docs/RT21_SETUP_PARITY.md`. Writes stay off until Andrew has approved each verified group on the real unit: read everything, back it up, change one harmless setting, read it back, and restore.
